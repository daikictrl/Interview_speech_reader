Microphone Access Required

Windows is blocking microphone hardware: While your browser shows a mic icon, Windows Privacy Settings has blocked desktop applications from accessing the audio device.

To enable live voice tracking and real-time scrolling:

  1. Press Win + I to open Windows Settings.
  2. Go to Privacy & Security → Microphone.
  3. Turn ON the main toggle: "Microphone access".
  4. Scroll down and turn ON: "Let desktop apps access your microphone" (or Google Chrome / Edge).
  5. In your browser address bar next to http://localhost:8080, click the tune/padlock icon and confirm Microphone is set to Allow.
